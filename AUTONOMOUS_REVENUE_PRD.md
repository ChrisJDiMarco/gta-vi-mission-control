# Autonomous revenue PRD

Date: 2026-06-27
Owner: Chris
Operator: JARVIS
Surface: `projects/gta-vi-mission-control`

## Decision

Use the GTA VI attention wave as the first autonomous-money sandbox. Do not start with trading, dropshipping, paid ads, crypto, gambling, scraped leaks, account sales, or anything that spends money without approval.

The first loop is a service business, not a passive-income fantasy:

1. Find small GTA-adjacent creators who already post public content.
2. Draft a paid clipper/trend-desk offer for them.
3. Produce sample hooks, captions, and content angles from official/public material.
4. Ask Chris to approve outbound before any message is sent.
5. Track replies, pilots, revenue, and follow-up tasks in GTA VI Mission Control.

## Why this lane

Rockstar lists GTA VI as coming November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That gives a dated attention window, not a vague trend. The opportunity is selling shovels to creators and local businesses riding the wave, without needing Chris to play the game.

Short-form ad monetization is a bad first revenue target. YouTube's public YPP path requires either 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months, or 1,000 subscribers plus 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days. YouTube also excludes non-original Shorts such as unedited clips or reposted compilations from Shorts monetization. That makes direct service revenue and paid pilots the faster path.

Affiliate revenue can be useful later, but every affiliate or sponsor relationship needs clear disclosure. The FTC says material connections, including payment, free products, discounts, or affiliate upside, must be disclosed clearly and near the endorsement. No buried disclosure, no vague "collab" language.

## Success criteria

- One approved offer for a GTA VI creator service.
- One prospect list with at least 50 qualified creators under 50K followers.
- One sample Trend Wire issue with hooks, formats, and caption drafts.
- One legal checklist attached to the workflow before outbound.
- Chris approves before the first external message, purchase, signup, affiliate link, or public post.

## Scope

In scope:

- Research public creators, public posts, platform policies, and legal guardrails.
- Draft offers, sample emails/DMs, scripts, hooks, captions, buyer guides, and content calendars.
- Build or modify local tools that store opportunities, tasks, scorecards, and receipts.
- Generate local reports Chris can approve.

Out of scope until Chris approves:

- Sending DMs, emails, comments, or posts.
- Buying domains, tools, ads, subscriptions, or data.
- Creating accounts or applying for affiliate programs.
- Using leaked footage, ripped assets, Rockstar logos, fake merch, exploits, account sales, currency sales, or boosting.
- Handling customer money, Stripe, taxes, or contracts.

## First 48-hour runbook

Hour 0-2: Produce a one-page offer for "GTA VI Launch Week Clip Desk" with three packages: $299/mo, $499/mo, and $799/mo. Keep the promise narrow: cut creator-owned streams into Shorts/Reels/TikToks, write titles/captions, and deliver a weekly clip queue.

Hour 2-4: Build a qualified prospect list of 50 small creators. Filters: posts GTA, gaming news, open-world games, streaming, or launch prep; under 50K followers; visible business email or public DM; recent activity in the last 30 days.

Hour 4-6: Draft one sample Trend Wire issue. Include five hooks, three video formats, ten captions, and one "do not touch" legal note.

Hour 6-8: Prepare outbound variants. Keep them plain: one DM, one email, one follow-up. No false scarcity, no fake partnership, no claims about guaranteed views.

Day 2: Chris reviews the offer, the prospect list, and the outbound copy. If approved, send the first batch manually or through a permissioned mail/social tool.

## Metrics

Track these in GTA VI Mission Control or a local markdown receipt:

- Prospects found.
- Prospects approved.
- Messages approved by Chris.
- Messages sent.
- Replies.
- Calls booked.
- Paid pilots.
- Revenue collected.
- Hours spent.
- Cost spent.

Kill or revise the loop if 50 approved prospects produce zero replies, or if the first paid pilot cannot be fulfilled cleanly with creator-owned assets.
